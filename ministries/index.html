Ministries We Sponsor

Our ministries today

Call to Return Ministries

Call to Return Ministries is a ministry of Midwest Hebrew Ministries, co-presided over by Tov and Michelle Rose. It carries the call of Romans 1:16 outward — proclaiming Yeshua to Israel and the nations and calling God’s people back to the foundations of the faith.

NMV Bible Project

The NMV Bible Project (NMV Bible Project & Foundation) is the Bible translation work of Midwest Hebrew Ministries, producing the New Messianic Version — a translation that restores the Jewish context, names, and idiom of the Scriptures for English readers.

Visit nmvbible.org →
