Shalom Y’all!

A greeting from Midwest Hebrew Ministries

Shalom! It means peace — wholeness, completeness, nothing missing and nothing broken. It is the greeting on our lips and the promise at the heart of everything we do.

To use an old cliché; “The world is sitting on a powder keg!” With everything happening in the Middle East — Israel in the headlines day after day, wars and rumors of wars — it is easy to wonder what God is doing and where this is all heading. Fear sells newspapers, but it is a terrible foundation for a life.

The God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob is not wringing His hands. The same God who brought His people back to their land is still writing the story, and the center of that story is Yeshua — Jesus — the long-awaited Messiah of Israel, who removed our transgressions as far as the east is from the west.

That is why Midwest Hebrew Ministries has existed since 1939: to share this shalom — first to the Jew, then to the Gentile (Romans 1:16). Whether you are Jewish and curious about the Messiah, or a believer wanting to understand the Jewish roots of your faith, you are welcome here. Pull up a chair.

Shalom y’all — and welcome.
