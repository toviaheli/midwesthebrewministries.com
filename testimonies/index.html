Testimonies

“For I am not ashamed of the gospel, because it is the power of God that brings salvation to everyone who believes: first to the Jew, then to the Gentile.”

— Romans 1:16

The gospel changes lives — that is the testimony of this ministry since 1939. Jewish lives turned to Yeshua the Messiah; believers awakened to the Jewish roots of their faith; prayers answered in Israel and in the Midwest.
